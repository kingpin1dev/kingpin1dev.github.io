Music downloader for Windows

Any link in, tagged music out.

Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp link, search for a track, or read a Spotify album or playlist. SpotiDrop downloads the audio, adds the title, artist, album and cover art, and saves it where Spotify's Local Files picks it up. yt-dlp and ffmpeg come bundled, so there is nothing else to install.

From link to Local Files

Spotify tab

Paste a track, album or playlist link. SpotiDrop reads the public track list and cover, then finds the audio on YouTube or SoundCloud.

Search and links

Search for a song, or paste a link from YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and other supported sites, playlists included.

Tags and cover art

Title, artist, album, track number and cover art are written into every file.

Spotify Local Files

Files are saved straight into your Local Files folder, ready to play in Spotify.

Queue

Download whole albums at once. Several tracks run in parallel with live progress.

Tag files you own

Already have the audio? Point SpotiDrop at a folder and it matches and tags the files with Spotify's metadata.

Updates itself

The yt-dlp engine can be refreshed from inside the app, and a new version shows a banner.

No account

Nothing to sign in to. Audio is never taken from Spotify; only its public metadata is read.

How it works

  1. 1

    Paste a link

    A Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp link, or search by name.

  2. 2

    Press Download

    SpotiDrop finds the audio, converts it and writes the tags and cover.

  3. 3

    Play in Spotify

    The files appear in Local Files, and in the app's Library tab.

Questions

Does it download audio from Spotify?

No. Spotify is only read for track names, artists and cover art. The audio comes from YouTube or SoundCloud.

Is it free?

Yes, and there is no account to create.

Which formats can I get?

MP3 at 44.1 kHz by default, with FLAC and other formats in Settings.

What may I download?

Only content you have the right to download. Please respect each platform's terms.

Ready to try SpotiDrop?

Download the installer and set it up in a minute. Use it only for content you have the right to download.
