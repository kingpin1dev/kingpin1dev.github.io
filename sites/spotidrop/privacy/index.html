SpotiDrop privacy policy

What SpotiDrop does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • SpotiDrop does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • SpotiDrop does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • SpotiDrop does not require an account.
  • Your settings, library and downloaded files stay on your device.

What SpotiDrop stores on your device

Your settings, your library list and the files you download are stored on your PC, in the folders you choose and in your user profile. Uninstalling SpotiDrop removes the app.

What SpotiDrop contacts to do its job

When you download or search, SpotiDrop (through the bundled yt-dlp) connects to the site you point it at, for example YouTube, SoundCloud or Bandcamp, to find and download audio. Those sites see your internet address and have their own privacy practices.

When you paste a Spotify link, SpotiDrop reads public Spotify pages for the track information and cover. It does not log in to Spotify and does not download Spotify's audio.

On start SpotiDrop fetches a small update manifest (latest.json) from kingpindev.com to see whether a new version exists, and it can download the yt-dlp engine from its public releases on GitHub. Like any web server, those servers may keep standard request logs (such as your internet address and the time of the request). We do not use them to identify you.

Thumbnails may be loaded from the image servers of the site you search.

Responsible use

Download only content you have the right to download. You are responsible for following each platform's terms and the law.

Children

SpotiDrop does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
