Getting started

What is SpotiDrop?

SpotiDrop downloads music from YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and other supported sites into your Spotify Local Files folder, tagged with title, artist, album and cover art, ready to play.

What do I need to install?

Nothing else. The installer bundles yt-dlp and ffmpeg. You need Windows 10 or 11 and an internet connection. The installer is about 122 MB, installs for your user and needs no administrator rights.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

SpotiDrop is free and there is no account to create.

Where do I get it?

From the download button on this site (kingpindev.com/spotidrop). It is not on the Microsoft Store.

Spotify

Does it download audio from Spotify?

No. Spotify is only read for public track information: title, artists, album, cover, duration, track number and release date. The audio comes from a public source such as YouTube or SoundCloud, or from files you already own.

Why is Spotify audio never downloaded?

Spotify streams are DRM-protected and licensed per country. SpotiDrop deliberately stops at the metadata.

What can I paste in the Spotify tab?

A Spotify track, album or playlist link, or a spotify: URI. Artist links are not supported.

Why does a track sometimes find nothing?

Some tracks are restricted by country or have no matching public source. The metadata still shows, but the source search may find nothing.

How do my files get into Spotify?

Files go into the folder you choose. The Spotify Guide in the app walks you through Settings, Local Files, Add a source, so the tracks play in Spotify and sync to your phone over Wi-Fi.

Downloads

Which formats can I get?

MP3 at 44.1 kHz by default, with FLAC and other formats in Settings. Spotify downloads are 44.1 kHz MP3 with the Spotify tags and a 500 by 500 cover.

Can I download a whole album?

Yes. Download all queues the whole album or playlist; several tracks run in parallel with live progress.

Can I tag files I already own?

Yes. Point SpotiDrop at a file, or use Match a folder to match a whole album by duration and name, and it writes Spotify's tags and cover.

What if YouTube rejects the download engine?

YouTube sometimes rejects older yt-dlp builds. SpotiDrop keeps the engine up to date and updates it once automatically when a download is rejected. There is also a manual Update in Settings.

Responsible use

What may I download?

Only content you have the right to download: Creative Commons, public domain, podcasts, your own uploads. Downloading copyrighted music may violate a platform's terms and copyright law. You are responsible for how you use SpotiDrop.

Privacy and support

Does SpotiDrop collect my data?

No accounts, analytics or advertising. See the privacy page for what it contacts to do its job.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to admin@kingpindev.com with your Windows version, your SpotiDrop version and what you were doing.
