Getting started

What is Aurora Radios?

Aurora Radios is a Windows app for listening to live radio from around the world. Pick a mood, genre or country, or search, and start a station with one click.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11. It needs an internet connection to stream stations.

Where do I get it?

From the Microsoft Store. Updates arrive automatically through the Store.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start listening.

Are there ads?

No. Aurora Radios shows no ads.

Finding stations

How do I find something to listen to?

Browse by mood (Chill, Focus, Energetic, Party, Sleep, Late Night and more), by genre, or by country, or type in the search box and results appear as you type.

Where do the stations come from?

Station information comes from the community-run Radio Browser directory, and the audio streams directly from each station.

Can I save stations?

Yes. Mark the stations you love as favourites for quick access.

Listening

Can I see which song is playing?

Yes. For stations that share it, the artist and title appear in the player.

What are liked songs?

Like a song and it is kept in your own list. From there you can search for it on Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music.

What is the Now playing view?

A full-screen view with album art and a live equalizer.

Does it keep playing when I close the window?

Yes. It keeps playing from the system tray until you stop it.

Does it work with Discord?

Optionally. If you turn it on, Aurora Radios can show what you are listening to as your Discord activity (Rich Presence).

Looks

Are there themes?

Two: a deep animated Aurora theme and a Windows 11 Mica theme.

Privacy and support

Does Aurora Radios collect my data?

No accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your favourites and liked songs stay on your PC. The app connects to the station directory and to the stations you play. See the privacy page.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to admin@kingpindev.com with your Windows version, your Aurora Radios version and the station you were playing.

How much does it cost?

The Store page always shows the current price.

Ready to try Aurora Radios?

Still have a question? Write to us at admin@kingpindev.com
