Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start listening.

Are there ads?

No, Aurora Radios shows no ads.

Can I see which song is playing?

Yes, for stations that share it, the artist and title appear in the player.

Does it keep playing when I close the window?

Yes, it keeps playing from the system tray until you stop it.
