Aurora Radios privacy policy

What Aurora Radios does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • Aurora Radios does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Aurora Radios does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • Aurora Radios does not require an account.
  • Your favourites, liked songs and settings stay on your device.

What Aurora Radios stores on your device

Your settings, favourite stations and liked songs are stored on your PC. Uninstalling Aurora Radios removes the app; you can also delete its data folder.

Network use

To work, Aurora Radios connects to the Radio Browser station directory to look up stations, and to the stations you choose to play to stream their audio. Those services see your internet address, like any website or radio player you use, and have their own privacy practices.

Aurora Radios does not send your listening history or favourites to our servers.

If you turn on Discord Rich Presence, Aurora Radios tells the Discord app on your PC which station you are listening to so Discord can show it as your activity. Discord's own privacy policy applies to it.

When you search for a liked song on Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music, the search opens in your browser and that service's privacy policy applies.

If you obtained Aurora Radios from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings and updates are handled by Microsoft through the services built into Windows.

Children

Aurora Radios does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
