Getting started

What is Mixora?

Mixora is a volume mixer for Windows that puts your apps into groups (Game, Chat, Media and Aux, plus your own) so you control volume, mute, equalizer and more for a whole group at once, instead of one app at a time.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and Windows 11.

Where do I get it?

From the Microsoft Store. Updates arrive automatically through the Store.

Do I need virtual audio devices or extra drivers?

No. Mixora works with Windows' own per-app audio. Per-group output devices use the devices you already have.

Was Mixora called something else?

Yes. It used to be called Groupy. It is the same app, now with an equalizer, auto-lowering and more.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español and Português (Brasil). You can switch language inside the app without restarting.

Groups and apps

How do I put an app in a group?

Apps that play sound show up in the detected apps list as you start them. Drag one onto a group, or click it and pick a group from the menu.

What about apps I open later?

A group's volume and mute apply to every app assigned to it, including ones that start after you set the level. Mixora can also recognise many well-known apps and game folders and suggest a group for them.

Can I make my own groups?

Yes. Besides Game, Chat, Media and Aux you can add custom groups (up to eight in total), and choose their name, colour and icon.

What happens to apps I close?

Closed apps move to an Inactive apps drawer under their group and come back to the group when they start again.

Can I ignore an app?

Yes. Apps you ignore are left out of the groups and the suggestions.

Sound

Does each group have its own equalizer?

Yes. Every group has a 10-band equalizer you can drag, plus bass, voice and treble sliders and a preamp. There are more than 350 ready profiles, favourites and your own saved presets, and you can import Equalizer APO files.

What is the automatic profile?

Turn it on for the Game group and the equalizer follows the game you are playing, then goes back when you quit.

What are the compressor, surround and noise reduction?

A compressor keeps loud peaks under control for any group. Virtual surround for headphones has a mode and a distance control. Noise reduction for the Chat group cleans up incoming voice chat.

What is ChatMix?

One slider that trades game volume for chat volume. Each group sits on the game side or the chat side, and moving the slider fades one side as the other gets louder.

How does auto-lowering work?

When a friend speaks in Discord, the groups you choose are turned down and then come back smoothly. You pick the trigger group, how much to lower and how sensitive it is.

Can games and chat use different speakers or headphones?

Yes. Route each group to its own output device, for example game audio to a headset and chat to speakers.

Do the volume sliders sound even?

Yes. Since 1.1.1 the sliders follow how ears hear loudness, so each step sounds even, and 0% is truly silent.

Hotkeys and the tray

Do the hotkeys work while I'm in a game?

Yes, they are global hotkeys. F13 to F24 work on their own; other keys need Ctrl, Alt or Shift.

What do the hotkeys control?

Group volume, mute, ChatMix, the microphone and profiles. A small pop-up shows the new level and you can drag its slider.

Does Mixora run in the background?

Yes, in the system tray, and optionally at startup. The tray panel has a light, dark or system theme.

Privacy and support

Does Mixora collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or ads, and the app has no network code of its own beyond opening links in your browser. See the privacy page for details.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to mixora@kingpindev.com. Tell us your Windows version, your Mixora version (Settings, About) and what you were doing.

How much does it cost?

Mixora is a paid app on the Microsoft Store. The Store page always shows the current price.
