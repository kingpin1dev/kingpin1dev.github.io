One line

Mixora is a Windows volume mixer that controls your apps by groups, with an equalizer for each group.

Short description

Mixora puts your Windows apps into Game, Chat, Media and Aux groups. Control volume, mute and an equalizer for a whole group at once, lower the game when a friend speaks, balance game and chat with ChatMix, and send each group to its own output device, with no virtual drivers.

Full description

Mixora is a volume mixer and equalizer for Windows 10 and 11. Instead of hunting through the Windows mixer one app at a time, you assign running apps to Game, Chat, Media and Aux groups (or groups of your own) and control each group together: volume, mute, a drag-able 10-band equalizer with more than 350 ready profiles, a compressor, virtual surround and noise reduction for voice chat. Auto-lowering turns the game down while a friend speaks in Discord, ChatMix balances game and chat from one slider, and each group can use its own output device without any virtual device or driver. Global hotkeys and a small tray panel keep everything within reach, even in a full-screen game. Mixora speaks English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español and Português (Brasil), has no account, ads or analytics, and is available on the Microsoft Store.
