Mixora privacy policy

What Mixora does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • Mixora does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Mixora does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • Mixora does not require an account.
  • Everything the app needs stays on your device.

What Mixora reads on your PC

To mix your apps, Mixora reads which apps are playing sound and their volume levels through the audio features built into Windows, and changes their volume, mute and output device when you ask it to.

The equalizer, compressor, surround and noise reduction process sound on your PC. Mixora does not send audio anywhere.

What Mixora stores on your device

Your settings, groups, app assignments, profiles, equalizer presets and shortcuts, in your Windows user profile. Uninstalling Mixora removes them.

Network use

Mixora has no network code of its own. It does not contact our servers.

If you obtained Mixora from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings and updates are handled by Microsoft through the services built into Windows.

Mixora contains links (for example to this website or to send an email). Opening a link uses your default browser or email app, and the site you visit has its own privacy policy.

Children

Mixora does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at mixora@kingpindev.com.
