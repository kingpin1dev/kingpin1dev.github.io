How it works

1

Open Mixora

Apps that play sound, like games, Discord or Spotify, appear in the detected apps list.

2

Assign them

Drag each app onto Game, Chat, Media or Aux, or click it and pick a group.

3

Control by group

Move one slider or press one hotkey and the whole group follows. Shape the sound with the equalizer.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need virtual audio devices or extra drivers?

No. Mixora works with Windows' own per-app audio, and per-group output devices use your existing devices.

What about apps I open later?

A group's volume and mute apply to every app assigned to it, including ones that start after you set the level.

Do the hotkeys work while I'm in a game?

Yes, they are global hotkeys. F13 to F24 work on their own; other keys need Ctrl, Alt or Shift.

What is ChatMix?

One slider that trades game volume for chat volume. Each group sits on the game side or the chat side, and moving the slider fades one side as the other gets louder.

Was Mixora called something else?

Yes. It used to be called Groupy. It is the same app, now with an equalizer, auto-lowering and more.
