GlideSnap privacy policy

What GlideSnap does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • GlideSnap does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • GlideSnap does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • GlideSnap does not require an account.
  • Its settings stay on your device.

What GlideSnap does on your PC

To work, GlideSnap listens for your mouse buttons, mouse wheel and the modifier key you chose, so it can move, resize, snap and fade the window under the pointer. It does this on your PC only. It does not record what you type or where you click, and it does not send that information anywhere.

It moves, resizes and changes the opacity or always-on-top state of other windows only when you use its gestures. It cannot control elevated or protected windows.

What it stores on your device

Your settings (keys, snapping and grid options, exclusions, theme) are stored in a small settings file on your PC. Uninstalling GlideSnap removes the app.

Network use

GlideSnap has no features that send data to our servers. Purchases, the trial, licensing, ratings and updates are handled by Microsoft through the services built into Windows. GlideSnap contains links (for example to our website or to send an email); opening a link uses your default browser or email app.

Children

GlideSnap does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
