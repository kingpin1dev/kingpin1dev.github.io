Getting started

What is GlideSnap?

GlideSnap is a small Windows tool that lets you move, resize, snap and make windows transparent from anywhere inside them, with a modifier key held (Alt by default), the way many Linux desktops do.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Where do I get it?

From the Microsoft Store. Updates arrive automatically through the Store.

Is there a trial?

Yes. The Store version has a 7-day trial; after that it needs a purchase. The Store page always shows the current price.

Do I need an account?

No.

Gestures

How do I move a window?

Hold Alt and drag with the left mouse button anywhere on the window, not just the title bar.

How do I resize a window?

Hold Alt and drag with the right mouse button. GlideSnap resizes from the corner or edge nearest to where you grabbed.

How do I change transparency?

Hold Alt and use the mouse wheel over a window.

How do I maximize or restore a window?

Hold Alt and double-click with the left button.

How do I keep a window on top?

Hold Alt and middle-click it. Do it again to unpin it.

Can I change the keys?

Yes. Choose Alt, Ctrl or Shift as the modifier and set what each mouse button does.

Snapping

How does snapping work?

Drop a window on the top edge to maximize it, or on the left or right edge to tile it to half the screen. Windows also align to screen and neighbouring window edges.

Are there grids?

Yes. Turn on 2 by 1, 3 by 1, 2 by 2 or 3 by 2 grids when you want them.

What happens to the size when I drag a snapped window out?

It gets its previous size back.

Compatibility

Does it work on every window?

On almost all of them. Windows does not let a normal app control elevated or protected windows.

Can I exclude an app?

Yes. Exclude by window title, window class or .exe name, or pause GlideSnap from the tray.

Does it run at startup?

Only if you turn that option on. It lives in the system tray.

Privacy and support

Does GlideSnap collect my data?

No accounts, analytics or advertising. It watches your mouse and keys locally to do its job and never records or sends them. See the privacy page.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to admin@kingpindev.com with your Windows version, your GlideSnap version and the app that misbehaved.
