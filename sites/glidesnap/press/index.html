One line

GlideSnap lets you move, resize and snap any Windows window from anywhere inside it with Alt and the mouse.

Short description

GlideSnap brings Linux-style window handling to Windows. Hold Alt and drag a window by any part of it to move it, drag with the right button to resize from the nearest corner, scroll for transparency, and drop it on an edge to snap it. It lives in the tray and stays out of the way.

Full description

GlideSnap is a lightweight window manager add-on for Windows 10 and 11. With Alt held (or the modifier you choose), a left drag moves a window from any point, a right drag resizes it from the nearest corner or edge, the mouse wheel changes its transparency, a double click maximizes or restores it and a middle click pins it on top. Dropping a window on an edge snaps it to full screen or half the screen, optional 2 by 1, 3 by 1, 2 by 2 and 3 by 2 grids are available, and a snapped window returns to its previous size when you drag it out. You can pick the modifier key and the action of each mouse button, exclude apps by title, class or .exe, and pause GlideSnap from the tray. It is a small native app with no account and is available on the Microsoft Store with a 7-day trial.
