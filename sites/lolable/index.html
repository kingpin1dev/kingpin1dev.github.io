Try it here

A live Discord channel with Lolable in it. Run /profil, /sonmac, /live, /compare, /gunluk, /championpool, /leaderboard or /duo. The players and numbers are made up, and the bot replies in Turkish.

What Lolable does

Riot ID linking

/bagla links your Riot ID to your Discord account, with optional rank roles.

Rank and form

/profil is a paged card: league, form, performance, champions and a coach note.

Last match

/sonmac breaks your latest ranked game into summary, build, damage, economy and coach pages.

Live scouting

/live shows both teams, risk tags, streaks and ban suggestions while a game is running.

Compare

/compare puts rank, win rate, KDA, CS and gold side by side for two players.

Daily summary

/gunluk sums up the last 24 hours of ranked games with your best match.

Server leaderboard

/leaderboard ranks linked players by SoloQ, and /duo finds partners in a similar league.

Match feed

/feed posts match results, rank changes and streaks to a channel you pick.

Questions

Do I need to link my account?

Linking is needed for /me and the leaderboard. You can also look up any Riot ID with /profil.

Where does the data come from?

From Riot Games' public API.

Is it affiliated with Riot Games?

No. Lolable is not endorsed or supported by Riot Games.

Which language does it use?

Lolable replies in Turkish.
