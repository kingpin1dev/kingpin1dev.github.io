Lolable privacy policy

What Lolable does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • Lolable is a non-commercial Discord bot. It collects only what it needs to provide its features.
  • It does not sell, trade or share your data beyond what is needed to run the service.
  • It does not collect Discord message content or private messages.

Data Lolable may store

Discord user, server and (if you configure a feed) channel IDs; the Riot ID (game name and tag line) you link and the matching Riot PUUID; your platform and region; profile icon and summoner level; ranked snapshot data (tier, division, LP, wins, losses, recent win rate); the last seen match IDs used only to detect new matches for the feed; and minimal server settings such as feed channels.

Data Lolable does not collect

Discord message content, direct or private messages, usernames or display names, email addresses, real names, passwords or payment information.

How the data is used

Only to provide Lolable's features: profiles, last matches, live scouting, comparisons, leaderboards, daily summaries and feed notifications. It is never used for advertising, profiling, gambling, boosting, cheating or any abuse of competitive integrity.

Riot Games data

Game data is retrieved in real time from the Riot Games API and Data Dragon, with caching, and is subject to Riot's Developer Policies. Lolable is not endorsed or supported by Riot Games.

Deleting your data

/baglantikes deletes your account link, league history and feed tracking records on the current server. /verisil deletes your account links and related bot records on all servers. Messages already sent to Discord and server backups are not deleted by these commands. League history snapshots older than 90 days are removed automatically.
