Getting started

What is Lolable?

Lolable is a Discord bot for League of Legends communities. It shows rank and LP, form and KDA, champion mastery, your last match, live game scouting, comparisons and a server leaderboard, all as slash commands.

How do I add it to my server?

Use the Add to Discord button. You need the Manage Server permission on the server. Discord shows the permissions the bot asks for before you confirm.

Which language does it use?

Lolable replies in Turkish.

Is it affiliated with Riot Games?

No. Lolable is not endorsed or supported by Riot Games. League of Legends and Riot Games are trademarks of Riot Games, Inc.

Linking and profiles

Do I need to link my account?

Linking is needed for /me and the leaderboard. You can also look up any Riot ID with /profil.

How do I link my Riot ID?

Run /bagla with your Riot ID and tag. It can also give optional rank roles.

What does /profil show?

A paged card: league, form, performance, champion pool, observations, the last 24 hours and league history.

What does /sonmac show?

Your latest game: summary, items, damage and economy.

Live games and comparisons

What does /live do?

While a game is running it shows both teams with ranks, masteries and recent form, with the sample size behind each number. It works as long as Riot's Spectator API access allows it, and says so clearly when it does not.

What does /compare do?

It puts two linked players side by side and states each player's sample size.

What is the daily summary?

/profil includes the last 24 hours of ranked games with your best match.

What does the leaderboard show?

Linked players on your server ranked by SoloQ LP. /duo lists members of a similar rank.

Notifications

What is the match feed?

/feed posts match results, rank changes and streaks to a channel you pick. It needs the Manage Server permission.

Will it announce old matches?

No. The feed does not announce old matches when you set it up; it only reports matches that happen afterwards.

Your data

Where does the data come from?

From Riot Games' public API and Data Dragon, with caching and rate limiting that respects Riot's limits.

How do I remove my data?

/baglantikes deletes your account link, league history and feed tracking records on the current server. /verisil deletes your account links and related bot records on all servers. Messages already sent to Discord are not deleted by these commands.

What does Lolable store?

Only what its features need. See the privacy page for the list.

Support

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to admin@kingpindev.com with your server name, the command you used and what happened.
