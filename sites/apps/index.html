All the apps, in one place.

Windows apps and Discord bots built by Burak Pekel. Pick one to see what it does and try it live in your browser.

LunaDockWindows app

LunaDock sorts the files, folders and shortcuts on your Windows desktop into tidy stacks. Click a stack to fan it open; your files never move.

GroupyWindows app

Groupy puts your Windows apps into Game, Chat, Media and Aux groups so you can set each group's volume, mute and output device from one place.

VICE SonarWindows app

VICE Sonar puts SteelSeries GG Sonar controls one click away: channel volumes, output routing, ChatMix, EQ profiles and RGB, from a widget or tray popup.

vOrganizerWindows app

vOrganizer sorts the files in your Downloads, Desktop or any folder into Images, Documents, Videos, Audio and more. Preview first, undo any time.

Aurora RadiosWindows app

Aurora Radios is an internet radio player for Windows. Find live stations by mood, genre or country, see the song that's playing and keep favourites.

SpotiDropWindows app

SpotiDrop downloads music from YouTube, SoundCloud and Bandcamp, tags it with cover art and drops it into Spotify's Local Files folder.

GoliathDiscord bot

Goliath is a multi-purpose Discord bot: moderation, levels, music and radio, a help panel and handy tools for your server, all as slash commands.

LolableDiscord bot

Lolable shows League of Legends ranks, last-match breakdowns, live match scouting and server leaderboards for your Discord community.

NotchifyWindows app

Notchify adds a Dynamic Island to your Windows screen.
