Desktop organizer for Windows

Your desktop, sorted into tidy stacks.

LunaDock gathers the files, folders and shortcuts on your Windows desktop into neat stacks: images, movies, music, documents, games, apps and more. Click a stack to fan it open, grab what you need, and click again to tuck it away. Your files stay exactly where they are.

Everything on your desktop, in the right place

Automatic stacks

Images, movies, music, documents, games, apps and more each get their own stack. Anything new you save to the desktop lands in the right one by itself.

By type or by date

Group by file type, or by date to see what is new at a glance: Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 Days and older.

Sorted your way

Sort inside each stack by name, date, size or type, and drag and drop to reorder stacks or move items between them.

20+ icon packs

Classic piles that show your files' own icons, or colorful category icons in more than 20 packs, from Fluent and Glass to Neon, Pastel and Aurora. Any stack can take your own icon or image.

Any edge, any monitor

Place the stacks on the top, bottom, left or right edge of the screen, with multi-monitor support. Adjust icon size, margins and background to match your wallpaper.

Works like Windows

Open, rename and delete with the familiar Windows right-click menu, and get a quick preview for files.

Out of the way

The original desktop icons are hidden while LunaDock runs and come back when it closes. Stacks open inline or in a panel next to the stack.

Made for Windows 11

A translucent settings window with a live preview, a guided welcome tour, light and dark mode, and 16 languages.

Questions

Does LunaDock move or change my files?

No. Your files stay where they are on the desktop. LunaDock only displays them in stacks, and it only renames or deletes something when you ask it to from its menu.

What happens when I close LunaDock?

The original desktop icons are hidden while LunaDock runs and come back as soon as it closes.

Can I use my own icons?

Yes. Choose from more than 20 icon packs, or give any stack its own icon or image.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes, LunaDock supports multiple monitors, and the stacks can sit on any edge of the screen.

Does it start with Windows?

It can. Starting with Windows is an option in the settings.
