LunaDock privacy policy

What LunaDock does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • LunaDock does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • LunaDock does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • LunaDock does not require an account.
  • Everything the app needs stays on your device.

Information processed on your device

To organize your desktop, LunaDock reads the names, types, icons, thumbnails and dates of the files, folders and shortcuts on your Windows desktop. This information is used only to display your stacks and is never sent anywhere.

LunaDock stores the following on your device, in your Windows user profile: your settings (such as language, icon pack and stack position), your layout (the order of your stacks and any custom names or icons you choose) and log files (technical diagnostic messages, for example errors, that help troubleshoot problems; logs stay on your device and are only shared if you choose to send them to us yourself). You can delete this information at any time by uninstalling LunaDock.

LunaDock does not move, modify, upload or delete your desktop files unless you explicitly ask it to, for example by renaming or deleting an item from its menu.

Microsoft Store

If you obtained LunaDock from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings and app updates are handled by Microsoft. When LunaDock checks for updates or opens the rating dialog, it uses the Microsoft Store services built into Windows. This information is processed by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive any personal information about you from these services beyond the standard sales and usage reports Microsoft provides to developers.

Links

LunaDock contains links (for example to our website or to send an email). Opening a link uses your default browser or email app, and the website you visit is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children

LunaDock does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at lunadock@kingpindev.com.
