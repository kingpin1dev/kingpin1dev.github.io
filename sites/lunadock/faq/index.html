Getting started

What is LunaDock?

LunaDock is a desktop organizer for Windows. It gathers the files, folders and shortcuts on your desktop into tidy stacks (images, movies, music, documents, games, apps and more) that fan open when you click them.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11.

Where do I get it?

From the Microsoft Store. Updates arrive automatically through the Store.

How much does it cost?

LunaDock is a paid app on the Microsoft Store. The Store page always shows the current price.

Which languages does it speak?

16 languages, including English, Türkçe, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Русский, 中文, 日本語, हिन्दी and العربية. It follows your Windows language by default.

Your files

Does LunaDock move or change my files?

No. Your files stay where they are on the desktop. LunaDock only displays them in stacks, and it only renames or deletes something when you ask it to from its menu.

What happens to my desktop icons?

The original desktop icons are hidden while LunaDock runs and come back as soon as it closes.

What happens to new files I save to the desktop?

They join the right stack by themselves, by type or by date depending on how you group.

Can I open, rename and delete files from a stack?

Yes. LunaDock uses the familiar Windows right-click menu, so Open with, Send to, Properties and the rest are all there. Delete sends items to the Recycle Bin, and holding Shift deletes permanently.

Can I drag files in and out of stacks?

Yes. Drag files to any app, or drop them on the desktop or on a folder. The usual Windows rules apply: Ctrl copies, Shift moves and Alt creates a shortcut.

Stacks and looks

Can I group by date instead of type?

Yes. Group by file type, or by date to see Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 Days and older.

Can I change the icons?

Yes. Choose from more than 20 icon packs, from Fluent and Glass to Neon, Pastel and Aurora, or give any stack your own icon or image.

Can I put the stacks somewhere else on the screen?

Yes. They can sit on the top, bottom, left or right edge, and you can adjust icon size, margins and the background to match your wallpaper.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes. LunaDock supports multiple monitors, and you can choose which monitor shows the stacks.

Does it work with Wallpaper Engine, Rainmeter and similar tools?

It is built to coexist with them: LunaDock leaves room for desktop widgets near the stacks, and clicks on empty space can pass through to an interactive wallpaper.

Keyboard and speed

Is there a quick preview?

Yes. Press Space on a file for a large preview of images, text and code, video and audio, and folder contents. If you use QuickLook, LunaDock hands the preview to it.

Which keyboard shortcuts are there?

Arrow keys move between items, Enter opens, F2 renames, Delete moves to the Recycle Bin, Ctrl+A, C, X and V select, copy, cut and paste, F5 refreshes and Esc closes the stacks.

Does it start with Windows?

It can. Starting with Windows is an option in the settings.

Privacy and support

Does LunaDock collect my data?

No. There is no account, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking. Everything stays on your PC. See the privacy page.

What if LunaDock stops responding?

If something goes wrong, LunaDock logs the error, brings your original desktop icons back and restarts itself.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to lunadock@kingpindev.com with your Windows version, your LunaDock version (Settings, About) and what you were doing.
