Hotpad privacy policy

What Hotpad does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • Hotpad does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Hotpad does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • Hotpad does not require an account.
  • Your profiles and settings stay on your device.

What Hotpad stores on your device

Your profiles, key assignments and settings are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hotpad\macros.json. Updating or uninstalling Hotpad does not delete that file. Exporting a backup writes a JSON file to the place you choose.

What Hotpad reads

To work, Hotpad sees which keyboard a key came from and the key itself, but only the macro keyboard you selected is captured and its keys are never typed into other apps. The keys of your other keyboards are passed through untouched and are not stored. The text you assign to a key is stored only in your settings file.

The list of installed apps in the editor is read from Windows on your PC and is not sent anywhere.

What Hotpad connects to

Hotpad does not connect to the internet while it runs. The setup program downloads Microsoft's WebView2 runtime from Microsoft only if it is missing from your PC. The download button on this website is served by kingpindev.com, which, like any web server, may keep standard request logs (such as your internet address and the time of the request). We do not use them to identify you.

The Interception driver

If you choose to install it, the Interception keyboard driver (open source, LGPL-3.0) is installed with administrator approval. It runs on your PC and does not send data anywhere. Uninstall it with its own uninstaller if you no longer need it.

Children

Hotpad does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
