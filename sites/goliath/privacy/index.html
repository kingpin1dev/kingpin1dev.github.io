Goliath privacy policy

What Goliath does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • Goliath does not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • It stores only what its features need, as described below.
  • It has no website accounts and no analytics.

What Goliath stores

To provide its features Goliath keeps a database with information such as Discord user and server (guild) IDs, level and XP and message counts for the level system, warnings and moderation records, reminders, AFK status and the settings admins choose (for example log channels).

Goliath needs the Discord privileged intents for server members, message content and presence to count activity for levels, power moderation and logs and show statuses.

Logs

If server admins set up logging, Goliath posts events (for example deleted or edited messages, members joining or leaving, role and channel changes, moderation actions and voice activity) to the channel they choose. That content then lives in your Discord server and is governed by your server's settings and Discord's terms.

Music

When you play music, Goliath looks up and streams audio from YouTube, using the links or searches you give it, in the voice channel you are in.

Third parties

Goliath runs on Discord and relies on Discord's services. Features such as translate, weather and Wikipedia search send your query to the matching public service to get an answer. We do not sell or share your data otherwise.

Removing your data

Ask us at admin@kingpindev.com and we will remove the data Goliath holds about you or your server. Removing the bot from a server stops it from collecting new data there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
