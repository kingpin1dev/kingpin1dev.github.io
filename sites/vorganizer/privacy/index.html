vOrganizer privacy policy

What vOrganizer does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • vOrganizer does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • vOrganizer does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • vOrganizer does not require an account and works offline.
  • Everything stays on your device.

What vOrganizer does on your PC

When you pick a folder, vOrganizer reads the names and types of the files directly inside it to decide where each one belongs. When you press Organize Now it moves those files into category folders inside the folder you chose. It does not read file contents, and it does not delete files.

To make Undo possible it remembers which files it moved during the last run.

Network use

vOrganizer has no features that send your files or file names anywhere. If you obtained it from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings and updates are handled by Microsoft through the services built into Windows.

vOrganizer contains links (for example to our website or to send an email). Opening a link uses your default browser or email app, and the site you visit has its own privacy policy.

Children

vOrganizer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
