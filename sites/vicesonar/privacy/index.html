VICE Sonar privacy policy

What VICE Sonar does and does not do with your information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Kingpindev

Summary

  • VICE Sonar does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • VICE Sonar does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • VICE Sonar does not require an account.
  • Everything it needs stays on your device.

What VICE Sonar does on your PC

To control Sonar, VICE Sonar talks to the SteelSeries GG and Sonar services that run on your own computer. It reads and changes the channel volumes, mutes, ChatMix, output devices, EQ presets and lighting colours that you control through the widget.

To react to your volume hotkeys it watches the mouse wheel and the keys you assigned. It does not record what you type.

What it stores on your device

Your settings (theme, language, layout, transparency, position, visible channels and hotkeys) are stored on your PC. Uninstalling VICE Sonar removes the app; you can also delete its settings folder.

Network use

VICE Sonar does not send data to our servers. If you obtained it from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings and updates are handled by Microsoft through the services built into Windows.

VICE Sonar contains links (for example to our website or to send an email). Opening a link uses your default browser or email app, and the site you visit has its own privacy policy.

Independence

VICE Sonar is not affiliated with or endorsed by SteelSeries. SteelSeries, GG and Sonar are trademarks of their owners.

Children

VICE Sonar does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at admin@kingpindev.com.
