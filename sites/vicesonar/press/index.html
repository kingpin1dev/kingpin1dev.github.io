One line

VICE Sonar is a small always-on-top Windows widget that controls SteelSeries Sonar: volume, ChatMix, EQ and RGB.

Short description

VICE Sonar puts your SteelSeries Sonar controls in a compact widget: every channel's volume and mute, ChatMix, per-device EQ profiles and GameSense RGB, plus a bubble mode and 7 themes. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with SteelSeries.

Full description

VICE Sonar is a sleek, always-on-top desktop widget for Windows 10 and 11 that controls SteelSeries GG Sonar without opening the main app. It offers volume sliders and mute toggles for Master, Game, Chat, Media, Aux and Mic, a ChatMix balance that follows the hardware wheel, per-channel output device switching, your saved Sonar EQ presets for every device, and GameSense RGB lighting with presets. The window is borderless and draggable, can shrink to a floating bubble, hides from the taskbar, lives in the tray and remembers where you put it. Seven themes, a horizontal layout, adjustable transparency, volume hotkeys and 7 interface languages (Türkçe, English, Français, Русский, Deutsch, Español, العربية) make it fit any setup. The full version is on the Microsoft Store; a free Lite build is available on GitHub. VICE Sonar is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SteelSeries.
