Companion app for SteelSeries GG Sonar

Your Sonar controls, one click away.

VICE Sonar is a lightweight widget for SteelSeries GG Sonar. Change every channel's volume, route audio to different devices, balance game and chat, switch EQ profiles and set your RGB lighting from a floating bubble or a quick popup above the tray, without opening the full GG app.

How it works

  1. 1

    Run SteelSeries GG

    VICE Sonar finds Sonar on its own and notices when GG starts or stops.

  2. 2

    Click the tray icon

    The popup opens above the tray with your channels, EQ and RGB.

  3. 3

    Or float the bubble

    Keep the Bubble widget on screen for one-click control while you play.

Questions

Do I need SteelSeries GG?

Yes. VICE Sonar controls Sonar, so SteelSeries GG with the Sonar app has to be installed and running.

Does it replace the GG app?

No, it works alongside it. It gives you quick access to the controls you change most, without opening the full app.

Which devices does the RGB control support?

Lighting is set through GameSense, which covers all SteelSeries device types.

Is VICE Sonar made by SteelSeries?

No. It is an independent app by Kingpindev and is not affiliated with SteelSeries.
