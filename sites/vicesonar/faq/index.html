Getting started

What is VICE Sonar?

VICE Sonar is a small always-on-top widget for Windows that controls SteelSeries Sonar for you: channel volumes, mutes, ChatMix, EQ profiles and GameSense RGB, without opening the main SteelSeries GG window.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, and SteelSeries GG installed and running with the Sonar app active. VICE Sonar talks to Sonar, so it needs it.

Is it made by SteelSeries?

No. VICE Sonar is an independent app by Kingpindev and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SteelSeries.

Where do I get it?

From the Microsoft Store (the full version), or as the free Lite build from the project's GitHub releases.

Can I install it with winget?

Yes. The free Lite build is on the Windows Package Manager: run winget install Kingpindev.VICESonar.

What is the difference between Lite and the Store version?

Lite has master volume and mute, favourite EQ profiles, RGB lighting, bubble mode, the tray and 7 languages. The Store version adds ChatMix, every audio channel with output switching, and all EQ profiles.

Which languages does it speak?

Türkçe, English, Français, Русский, Deutsch, Español and العربية, with instant switching.

Audio and ChatMix

Which channels can I control?

Master, Game, Chat, Media, Aux and Mic, each with a volume slider and a mute toggle. Channels collapse and expand to keep the widget small.

Can I switch output devices?

Yes. Each channel has an output picker, so you can move Game, Chat, Media or Aux to another device on the fly.

How does ChatMix work?

A single slider balances Game against Chat and stays in sync with Sonar's own ChatMix, including the hardware wheel on supported SteelSeries headsets.

Do the volume hotkeys work in games?

Yes. You can assign a key combination per channel with the mouse wheel or arrow keys, choose the step (2, 5, 10 or 15 per cent) and assign a shortcut that opens the widget.

EQ and lighting

How do EQ profiles work?

VICE Sonar lists the EQ presets you already saved in Sonar for each device, with separate pickers for Game, Chat, Mic, Media and Aux. Pick one and it is applied. A Reload button refreshes the list.

How does RGB work?

R, G and B sliders with a live preview, one-click presets (Orange, Red, Blue, Green, White, Off) and a Hue picker. Colours are sent to your devices through GameSense.

The widget

What is bubble mode?

The widget shrinks to a small floating always-on-top circle. Click it to open the full panel again.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. There are 7 themes (Sunlight, Moonlight, Orbit, Aqua, Eris, Cyber and Spider Lily), a horizontal layout, and a transparency slider from 40 to 100 per cent.

Does it clutter my taskbar?

No. It has no taskbar entry. It lives in the system tray, and it remembers where you placed it.

Does it start with Windows?

It can. Start with Windows is an option in Settings.

Privacy and support

Does VICE Sonar collect my data?

No. There is no account, analytics or advertising. The widget talks to the Sonar service on your own PC and keeps its settings on your device. See the privacy page.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to admin@kingpindev.com with your Windows version, your VICE Sonar version (Settings, About) and your SteelSeries GG version.
