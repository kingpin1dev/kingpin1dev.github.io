Lalune

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lalune, developed by Kingpindev, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles user data.

Data and storage

Lalune stores your preferences, bookmarks, custom layouts, notes, and widget settings in your Chrome profile. Supported settings may be synchronized depending on your selected synchronization method.

Browser permissions

Lalune accesses Chrome bookmarks, website icons, and optionally your most visited websites and default search engine to provide its dashboard functionality.

Location and external services

Lalune uses Open-Meteo to provide weather information based on your selected location. It also connects to external services to retrieve wallpapers and fonts.

Data sharing

Lalune does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to its functionality.

External services receive information necessary to provide their respective features.

Data control

You can manage your preferences, export or delete saved data, and uninstall Lalune at any time.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through kingpindev.com.
