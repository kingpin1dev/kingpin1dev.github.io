LunaDock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

LunaDock is a desktop organizer for Windows, published by Kingpindev. It is designed to work entirely on your own computer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

In short: LunaDock does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, or accounts. Everything the app needs stays on your device.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

To organize your desktop, LunaDock reads the names, types, icons, thumbnails, and dates of the files, folders, and shortcuts on your Windows desktop. This information is used only to display your stacks and is never sent anywhere.

LunaDock does not move, modify, upload, or delete your desktop files unless you explicitly ask it to (for example, by renaming or deleting an item from its menu).

3. Information Stored on Your Device

LunaDock stores the following in your Windows user profile:

  • Settings – your preferences, such as language, icon pack, and stack position
  • Layout – the order of your stacks and any custom names or icons you choose
  • Log files – technical diagnostic messages (for example, errors) that help troubleshoot problems. Logs stay on your device and are only shared if you choose to send them to us yourself.

You can remove this information at any time by uninstalling LunaDock.

4. Microsoft Store

If you obtained LunaDock from the Microsoft Store, purchases, licensing, ratings, and app updates are handled by Microsoft. When LunaDock checks for updates or opens the rating dialog, it uses the Microsoft Store services built into Windows. This information is processed by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive any personal information about you from these services beyond the standard sales and usage reports Microsoft provides to developers.

5. Links

LunaDock contains links, for example to our website or to send us an email. Opening a link uses your default browser or email app, and the website you visit is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Children

LunaDock does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
